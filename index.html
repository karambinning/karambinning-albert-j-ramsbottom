From the counter Forty-four years of cod, chips, mushy peas & tea — cooked to order, served at the same address on Abington Street since the lights first came on in 1982.

A proper chippy is a quiet sort of place. The fryer hums, the tea brews, the chips come up golden — not skinny, not greasy, just chips the way chips should be. We’ve been doing it this way at 94 Abington Street for more than four decades, and we still cook everything to order, every order.

Fish from the sea, batter mixed fresh, chips cut and fried in clean oil. A dedicated gluten-free fryer for those who need it, vegetarian options for those who want them, and a fully licensed dining room for those who fancy a glass with their plaice. Or take it home in paper, the old-fashioned way.

No frills, no surprises — just the fish & chips Northampton has been queueing for since 1982.
